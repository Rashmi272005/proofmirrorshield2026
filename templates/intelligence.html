DETECTION ENGINE / 03

What the engine examines

Warning patterns checked by the current analysis modules.

✉ TEXT ANALYSIS

Message intelligence

Examines submitted text for selected language patterns associated with suspicious requests and pressure tactics.

  • Urgent demands and pressure language
  • OTP, password and PIN requests
  • Advance payment requests
  • Unrealistic reward claims
  • Threatening language
  • Remote access requests
  • Suspicious contact instructions
RULE-BASED TEXT CHECKS
↗ URL ANALYSIS

Link structure intelligence

Inspects the submitted web address for structural characteristics that may deserve additional attention.

  • HTTP versus HTTPS
  • Known URL shortener domains
  • Direct IP-address domains
  • Punycode domain indicators
  • Unusual numbers of subdomains
  • Misleading URL structures

RISK MODEL / 04

Understanding the risk score

How the current engine groups its calculated scores.

LOWER RISK 0–24

Fewer warning indicators were detected by the current checks. This does not guarantee safety.

NEEDS REVIEW 25–59

Potential warning indicators were detected. Review the findings before taking action.

HIGH RISK 60–100

Strong or multiple warning indicators were detected. Treat the content with caution.
