01 / OUR MISSION

Digital trust begins
with awareness.

Digital threats are becoming harder to recognise. Suspicious messages can create urgency, request sensitive information, or direct people to questionable websites.

ProofMirror Shield provides a structured first-level check of submitted text and URLs. It highlights selected warning patterns and organises the findings into an understandable report.

05 / HONEST BY DESIGN
i

Trust the process.
Understand its limits.

ProofMirror Shield currently uses rule-based pattern checks. It does not independently establish whether a message is true, confirm a sender's identity, or guarantee that a website is safe. A lower-risk result is not proof of authenticity. Always verify important information through trusted official channels.
