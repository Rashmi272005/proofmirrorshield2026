Risk Classification Guide

These ranges represent the score thresholds used by the current analysis engine.

LOWER RISK 0–24 Fewer warning indicators were detected. This does not guarantee authenticity.
NEEDS REVIEW 25–59 Warning indicators were found. Review the evidence carefully.
HIGH RISK 60–100 Strong warning indicators were detected. Avoid acting until independently verified.
